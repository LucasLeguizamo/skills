# Session 10 — Organic content that sells: acquisition, authority, conversion (Sesión 10 — IA y automatizaciones para contenido)

Problem: accounts that either only entertain (no sales) or only sell (become a catalog) → run an organic 3-pillar funnel (acquisition → authority → conversion), with a mix ratio set by account stage, plus conversion Stories and ManyChat keyword automations.

> Note: despite the program title ("AI and automations"), the class was taught by guest instructor Santiago Sánchez (Converso) and was about sales-oriented organic content strategy. Automation was limited to ManyChat; AI appeared only as an idea-generation exercise.

## Key ideas
- Social networks were not built for brands: people come for entertainment, information and connection. Hook first, build trust, then offer. "Engancho a la persona, le genero confianza y le ofrezco" (I hook the person, build trust, then offer — that is the proper process). Selling all the time turns the account into a catalog.
- Every algorithm evaluates two things: interactions and retention.
- **Pillar 1 — Acquisition** (top of funnel, brings new people): motivational/reflection, humor, entertainment, education and information. Formats that work: informative expert videos, no-sell tutorials, lists, "this is good / this is better" comparisons.
- Acquisition content must connect to what you sell; otherwise you grow an audience that never buys (example: a famous apparel brand whose winning content is motivational quotes, not the clothes).
- **Pillar 2 — Authority** (builds trust):
  - Testimonials: record conversationally, side by side, two microphones, camera not pointed head-on at the client (feels less staged).
  - Before / after / bridge (show the transformation and what got them there).
  - Achievements: include the community in the win; don't brag.
  - Events, and influential people appearing on your channels.
- **Pillar 3 — Conversion**:
  - Direct sale: only after you've "earned the ad slot" — like a TV soap that earns its commercial breaks.
  - Reviews.
  - Comparisons (there isn't always a winner; it depends on the buyer profile).
  - Indirect sale: problem → solution → CTA, using open loops.
- **Conversion Stories:** Stories lost reach and are mostly seen only by followers, but they remain a strong link/conversion channel because the viewer already knows you. Vary the dynamic instead of repeating one.
- Story dynamics that convert: emoji reaction then CTA later; keyword reply with ManyChat auto-DM; 3-story sequence (hook / interaction / CTA). Other variants: secret coupon, early-access drops, games, scarcity, price reveal, video, personalization, zero-friction purchase.
- Story design should look casual and organic, not hyper-produced.
- **ManyChat:** Meta has invested in the platform. Use "automatic DM links" triggered by comments on posts/Reels and by Story replies with a specific keyword. Register keyword variants (with and without accents, abbreviations) and several alternative replies so the bot doesn't look repetitive.
- ManyChat has a template to greet new followers; the instructor dislikes it (feels invasive).
- On TikTok, the equivalent DM automation is not yet available to everyone (beta, business accounts).
- Established account with no reach: first rule out bad practices (giveaways/contests, bought followers, fake engagement, long inactivity, mass follow/unfollow). If none apply, it is usually the content. Option: open a parallel account with focused (not duplicated) content.
- Paid: the instructor does not boost the profile itself ("son esteroides" — they're steroids); instead uploads the sales video file directly in Ads Manager.
- Before a launch: build a checklist per channel with the sales script for each lead type.
- Use AI as an idea engine: feed it a list of your audience's key needs (or the problems your product solves) and ask for content ideas.

## Numbers & rules of thumb
- Content mix for small accounts (~1,600–5,000 followers): 50% acquisition / 25% authority / 25% conversion.
- When growth is no longer the priority: 33% / 33% / 33%.
- Never drop acquisition below 33%.
- Informative expert video example: 277,000 likes, estimated ~50,000 followers gained.
- No-sell tutorial example: ~30,000 followers gained.
- Emoji-reaction Story case: 1,360 reactions → 1,174 organic link clicks → ~900 WhatsApp messages; CTA Story posted ~1 hour after the reaction Story.
- ManyChat keyword Story case: 6,275 automated replies → 574 clicks to the offer in the next Story.
- Hook / interaction / CTA 3-Story case: ~2,000 replies to the keyword → ~300 clicks.
- Stories per day: brand 6–7 (up to 10 if there is a narrative); influencers can post more.
- 30–50 Stories/day only works with real scarcity (e.g., boutiques with limited units).
- Rest 1–2 days per week from Stories, choosing your worst-performing days.
- Second instructor: ~99% of no-reach cases are content, not a penalty; ~100 videos is enough volume to tell whether the problem is the account or you.

## Frameworks / step-by-step methods
**Organic sales funnel**
1. Acquisition: hook new people with value/entertainment tied to what you sell.
2. Authority: build trust (testimonials, before/after/bridge, achievements, events, influencers).
3. Conversion: offer (direct sale, reviews, comparisons, indirect problem → solution → CTA).
4. Set the ratio by stage (50/25/25 small → 33/33/33 mature; acquisition ≥33%).

**Emoji-reaction Story**
1. Post a Story asking viewers to react with an emoji if they want X.
2. Wait ~1 hour.
3. Post the CTA Story with the link (or WhatsApp).

**Keyword Story with ManyChat**
1. Story invites viewers to reply with a keyword.
2. ManyChat auto-DMs the link/offer (register keyword variants + multiple reply texts).
3. Follow with a Story containing the offer link.

**3-Story conversion sequence**
1. Hook Story.
2. Interaction Story (poll/keyword reply).
3. CTA Story.

**Indirect sale video**: problem → solution → CTA, with open loops to hold retention.

**Diagnosing a no-reach account**
1. Check for bad practices (contests, bought followers, fake engagement, inactivity, mass follow/unfollow).
2. If clean, assume content is the problem; increase volume (~100 videos) and iterate.
3. Optionally test a parallel account with focused, non-duplicated content.

**Content ideation exercise**
1. List 10 key audience needs (or 10 problems your product solves).
2. Load the list into an AI chat to generate content ideas per pillar.

## Tools mentioned
- ManyChat — keyword-triggered auto-DMs from comments and Story replies (Instagram; TikTok in beta for business accounts). No price stated.
- Meta Ads Manager — upload the sales video file directly rather than boosting the profile. No budget stated.
- AI chat (generic) — idea generation from the needs/problems list.
- WhatsApp — destination for Story CTAs.

## Pitfalls / what instructors warned against
- Selling all the time → account becomes a catalog.
- Acquisition content unrelated to what you sell → followers who never buy.
- Humor can undermine professionalism in some niches.
- Trending audios with no voice/language can go viral in countries where you don't sell.
- Direct selling before earning the "ad slot" with value content.
- Hyper-produced Stories; posting dozens of Stories without real scarcity.
- Posting every day without rest; publishing on your worst days.
- Repeating the same Story dynamic over and over.
- Single-variant ManyChat keywords and identical bot replies (looks robotic); auto-greeting new followers (invasive).
- Boosting the profile ("steroids") instead of running ads on a specific sales video.
- Giveaways, bought followers, fake engagement, mass follow/unfollow, long inactivity — they damage reach.
- Blaming an algorithm "penalty" when the content is the usual cause; there is no single right way, judge case by case.

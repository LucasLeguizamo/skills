# social-content-30x — volume with structure

**Thesis:** volume with structure beats planning, and the market — not the creator — decides what works.
Every rule below is a layer on top of that. When in doubt, the answer is almost always: *publish more*.

The program's order matters and so does this skill's: **unblock → get permission to copy → only then add identity and tools.**
Identity is not the starting point; it shows up once there is volume. Do not let a user automate, build an avatar or polish a brand before they have published.

## The operating system

1. **Publish every day.** Horizon: **100 videos** (~3 months). Judge yourself at video 100, not video 2. Cruising pace: 5–7 per week.
2. **Don't invent — find references.** Search the niche keyword in TikTok/Instagram search, in Spanish, English and Portuguese. Filter for organic reach by **likes, never views** (views include paid). Prefer simple formats from accounts that are not huge.
3. **Deconstruct the reference** into variables: idea, length, location, camera position, lighting, audio, tone. Copy the format, **not the person**.
4. **Don't touch the hook.** Copy the first 3 seconds as-is (on-screen text, typeface, music, framing). Most production time goes here.
5. **Cut the silences.** The most important edit by far — above effects and typography.
6. **Post to TikTok first** (the lab: the algorithm doesn't forgive, so it teaches), then replicate to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn.
7. **Measure at 7 days minimum**, never after one. Compare against yourself last week, not against other creators.
8. **When something works, squeeze it:** re-film it the same way, make part 2, turn it into a series.
9. **Only once a template is stable, automate it.**

## Numbers

- **100 videos** — horizon to find identity
- **5–7 videos/week** — cruising pace
- **60/10** — 60% of production time on the first 10 seconds
- **< 2 s** — to break the scroll pattern
- **2X rule** — the bar is not "they watched it", it's "they watched it twice"
- **10% → 50% → new audience** — the distribution filters
- **3–4 pillars max** — more and the audience stops knowing what you are
- **90/10** — 90% under your rules, 10% pure experimentation (session 12 says 80/20)
- **80/10/10** — more of the same / improved / new
- **Funnel mix** — small account 50% acquisition / 25% authority / 25% conversion; otherwise 33/33/33, never under 33% acquisition
- **Reference floor** — 15k–20k likes (S3) or 20k–30k (S5); what really matters is the likes/views ratio
- **Length** — S3 says 24–40 s, S4 40–45 s, S5 15 s–1:30. No single number: not one second should be spare.
- **Cadence** — baseline 1/day (S2, S4, S12); S6 pushes 2–4/day.
- **Restart the account?** — thresholds range from under ~2–3K followers (S2, S3) to under 10–20K (S4, S5). Default: keep the account, fix the content.
- **Hashtags** — S5: at most 3. S12: none, keywords in the description.

## How to use this skill

- **Plan a calendar:** pick 3–4 pillars (S6), fill 80–90% with proven formats from references (S4–S5), reserve 10–20% for tests. Daily posting goal, measured weekly.
- **Write a script:** hook copied from a working reference → customer as hero, creator as guide (S7) → one idea → CTA only if it's a conversion piece (S10). Cut every silence.
- **Diagnose "no reach":** it's almost always the content, and the fix is volume — not a new account, not hashtags (use keywords in the description instead), not paid on the organic profile. Check: posting volume, hook in < 2 s, retention, format copied from a validated reference.
- **Sell organically:** acquisition content must connect to what you sell (a trend with no language goes viral with no direction); authority = testimonials, wins, community, events; conversion = few stories per day with a reaction or keyword CTA, automated DMs (ManyChat) on the exact keyword plus variants (S10).
- **Scale:** manual first. The program's own team did ~600 cases by hand with a spreadsheet before automating (S8). Personal brand before company account; keep organic and paid separate; outsource paid.
- **Hire:** hire what you need, not what you want. Six skills — strategist, filming, editing, content manager, metrics, design — with AI as a cross-cutting layer; ~5 people is ideal. First hire: videographer + editor, or a young right hand (S11).

## Push back on

- Expecting results in two weeks. The horizon is 100 videos.
- "I need more information." The number one piece of information: you don't need more information.
- Automating, building an AI avatar or opening a second account before having volume. Session 12 closes with "no AI avatars to connect" and back to volume.
- Judging a video in a day, or against someone else's account.
